About Us

The VIP treatment, every visit.

Pampered Pets is an independent grooming parlour on Selsdon Road, South Croydon. We've been here around ten years, and plenty of our four-legged clients have been coming nearly that long. Customers tell us their pets are "treated as a VIP" here, and honestly, that's the whole idea.

Our groomers are trained, patient and properly pet-obsessed. Jess, our senior groomer, has looked after some of the same dogs for years. Whether it's a bouncy first-time puppy, a dignified older dog, or a cat who has strong opinions about baths, they all get the same calm, careful attention.

  • ✦ Independent and established: around a decade serving South Croydon.
  • ✦ Trained, patient groomers who take their time with every pet.
  • ✦ Dogs and cats: one of the few local parlours that grooms both.

🐱 Yes, Cats Too

One of the few parlours around that properly grooms cats.

Most grooming salons are dogs-only. We're not. Long-haired cats especially benefit from regular professional grooming: it prevents painful matting, cuts down on hairballs, and keeps their coat in far better condition than brushing at home can manage alone.

Cats are handled differently to dogs, with a quieter pace and a lot of patience. If your cat has never been professionally groomed before, give us a call and we'll talk you through how it works.
